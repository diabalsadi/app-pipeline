# Decision record template

Write one record per decision in `docs/04-decisions/NN-<topic>.md`, for example `01-app-framework.md`. Write them for a non-technical reader: explain any technical term in one short phrase.

```markdown
# [NN]. [Decision, for example "App framework"]

**Decision:** [the choice, in one sentence]
**Status:** Proposed. It becomes Accepted when the founder approves the architecture.

## What this decides
[One or two sentences on what this part of the app does, in plain language.]

## Options
| Option | Good | Not so good | Monthly cost at launch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Option A] (recommended) | [plain words] | [plain words] | [figure with a link, or "free tier"] |
| [Option B] | | | |

## Why the recommendation
[2 or 3 sentences tied to the business plan or requirements, such as the budget, platform, data rules, or scale.]

## What would change our mind
[The condition that would make another option better, for example "more than 50,000 monthly users".]
```
