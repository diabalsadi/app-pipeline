# Region pack · United Arab Emirates
Owner: Library Curator · Version 1.0 · Last reviewed: 2026-10 · Verify legal facts before relying on them.

## Signals
- Majority expatriate population: English is the working language for most consumer and B2B products; Arabic is required for government-facing and Emirati-focused services and expected as an option elsewhere. Large South Asian and Filipino communities.
- Weekend Saturday–Sunday since 2022 (Friday is a shorter working day in government). Ramadan reduces working hours.
- Very high digital adoption; national digital identity (UAE Pass) is standard for government and many regulated services.
- Payments: cards and Apple Pay dominate; BNPL (Tabby, Tamara) common; cash on delivery declining but still offered.
- Emirate-level differences: Dubai, Abu Dhabi and others have their own regulators and systems (e.g. health authorities, address systems).
- Premium, polished service expectations; fast delivery norms.

## Do
- English + Arabic, with Arabic fully RTL; decide which is default per audience and state it.
- Prices in AED with two decimals. A new dirham symbol was introduced in 2025; check font support and the central bank's usage guidance before using it, otherwise "AED" / "د.إ".
- Phone format +971 5X XXX XXXX; support UAE Pass sign-in where identity matters.
- Specify the Emirate(s): rules, regulators and address systems differ.
- Represent the real multinational audience in content and imagery.

## Don't
- Assume Friday–Saturday weekend (that is Saudi/Jordan, not UAE).
- Treat "the UAE" as one regulator for health, education or free zones.
- Store health data outside the UAE without checking the health data law's exceptions.

## References
- Identity & government: UAE Pass, TAMM (Abu Dhabi), DubaiNow; Makani (Dubai address system).
- Payments: cards, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara; instant payments (Aani); regulator Central Bank of the UAE.
- Data protection: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL); DIFC and ADGM free zones have their own data-protection laws; health data under Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 (ICT in health fields) with data-localisation rules.

## Tokens
locale en-AE / ar-AE · dir ltr (English) / rtl (Arabic) · currency AED (2 decimals) · weekend Sat–Sun · phone +971 · date DD/MM/YYYY.

## Checks
- Both languages complete; Arabic passes RTL mirror test.
- Correct weekend in schedules, SLAs and reports.
- Data hosting decision recorded per data type (personal, health, financial).
