# Industry pack · Education (universities, schools, training)
Owner: Library Curator · Version 1.0 · Last reviewed: 2026-10 · Verify legal facts before relying on them.

## Signals
- Three very different audiences: students (mobile, informal, deadline-driven), faculty/teachers (time-poor, desktop for heavy tasks), administrators (dense data, reports, approvals). Parents matter in schools and often in university decisions too.
- Bilingual by nature in the region: many university programmes teach in English while administration runs in Arabic.
- Academic calendars, exam periods and Ramadan drive peaks; results day and registration week are high-stress moments.
- Hierarchy and titles matter (Dr., Prof., Dean); approvals chain through supervisors and departments.
- Student data includes minors in schools — handle with extra care.

## Do
- Design per role: quick mobile flows for students, efficient desktop flows for staff, reports for admins.
- Support both Arabic and English for official records (names, transcripts) where required.
- Map approvals to the institution's real hierarchy (supervisor → department → dean).
- Plan for peak loads at registration and results.
- Use the institution's real faculty names, course codes and building names in content.

## Don't
- One-size-fits-all dashboards for every role.
- Informal tone in official notices (grades, discipline, finance).
- Collect more student data than needed, especially for minors.

## References
- Jordan: Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research; Tawjihi (secondary school exam).
- Saudi Arabia: Ministry of Education; Madrasati (school learning platform), Noor (school information system).
- UAE: Ministry of Education; KHDA (Dubai private education), ADEK (Abu Dhabi).
- Common tools users know: Moodle, Microsoft Teams, Google Classroom, Blackboard.

## Tokens
Roles: student · faculty · admin · parent (if schools) · official names in Arabic + English · academic year format e.g. 2026/2027.

## Checks
- Each role's top task is reachable in ≤ 3 taps/clicks.
- Official documents render both scripts correctly.
- Approval chain matches the institution's real structure.
