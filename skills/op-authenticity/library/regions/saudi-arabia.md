# Region pack · Saudi Arabia
Owner: Library Curator · Version 1.0 · Last reviewed: 2026-10 · Verify legal facts before relying on them.

## Signals
- Arabic-first society; government and many consumer services are Arabic-default with English. Large expatriate workforce (English, Urdu, Hindi, Tagalog, Bengali, Arabic dialects).
- Weekend Friday–Saturday. Ramadan and the two Eids reshape hours and peaks; Hijri calendar remains culturally important alongside Gregorian.
- Very high smartphone and digital-government adoption; national digital identity is the normal way to prove who you are.
- Card and mobile payments dominate; mada debit cards, Apple Pay and BNPL (Tabby, Tamara) are widespread.
- Strong preference for formal, respectful tone; family and gender considerations affect services (e.g. female staff options, family sections).
- Vision 2030 language is common in B2B and government contexts; Saudization (Nitaqat) shapes HR products.

## Do
- Arabic-first, RTL by default, with English; Gulf/Saudi-appropriate vocabulary in marketing, Modern Standard Arabic in formal UI.
- Prices in SAR with two decimals. A new Saudi riyal symbol was introduced in 2025; check font support before using it, otherwise write "SAR" / "ر.س".
- Support mada, Apple Pay and cards; consider SADAD for bills and BNPL where relevant.
- Use national digital identity (Nafath) for identity verification where the product needs it; phone format +966 5X XXX XXXX.
- Support the National Address format for deliveries and field work.
- Show Hijri dates alongside Gregorian where religious or government context expects it.

## Don't
- Assume Jordanian/Levantine wording or prices; don't reuse JOD formats.
- Imagery or copy that ignores modesty and family norms.
- Hosting regulated data outside the Kingdom without checking the rules.

## References
- Identity & government: Nafath (national SSO), Absher (government e-services), Tawakkalna.
- Payments: mada, SADAD, Apple Pay, STC Bank (formerly STC Pay), Tabby, Tamara; regulator SAMA.
- Data protection: Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), supervised by SDAIA, enforced from September 2024; cross-border transfer rules apply.
- HR: GOSI, Qiwa, Mudad (wage protection).

## Tokens
locale ar-SA / en-SA · dir rtl · currency SAR (2 decimals) · weekend Fri–Sat · phone +966 · date DD/MM/YYYY with optional Hijri · Arabic-Indic digits acceptable in Arabic UI if consistent.

## Checks
- Arabic RTL mirror test on every screen; Hijri/Gregorian display correct where used.
- mada and Apple Pay paths work end to end (if payments).
- Personal data flows documented against PDPL (consent, purpose, transfer).
