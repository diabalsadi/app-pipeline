# Region pack · Jordan (Levant)
Owner: Library Curator · Version 1.0 · Last reviewed: 2026-10 · Verify legal facts before relying on them.

## Signals
- Arabic is the everyday language; English is widely read by professionals, students and in business. Many users switch between the two (and write Arabic in Latin letters, "Arabizi", in chat).
- Working week Sunday–Thursday; weekend Friday–Saturday. Ramadan changes working hours and peak usage (late evening).
- Strong family role in decisions (health, education, purchases); formal respect for elders and titles (Dr., Eng., Abu/Umm + eldest child's name).
- Mobile-first; Android dominant in mass market, iPhone common among higher-income users. WhatsApp is the default channel for support and coordination.
- Addresses are often described by area + street + landmark ("near the 7th circle"); map pins are shared on WhatsApp.
- Cash is still common; instant payments via CliQ have grown fast; mobile wallets exist alongside cards.

## Do
- Arabic-first with English toggle (or English-first for professional/B2B audiences who state it), both fully supported.
- Show prices in Jordanian Dinar with three decimals (1 JD = 1,000 fils), e.g. "3.250 JD" / "3.250 د.أ".
- Offer CliQ and cash on delivery where consumers pay; card as an option. Consider eFAWATEERcom for bills.
- Mobile number + SMS code sign-in; phone format +962 7X XXX XXXX.
- Respect Friday (prayer time around midday) and Ramadan schedules in booking and notifications.
- Use local names, areas (e.g. Abdoun, Jubeiha, Khalda, Irbid, Zarqa) and real landmarks in content.

## Don't
- Month-first dates (use DD/MM/YYYY), dollar signs, imperial units.
- Gulf or Egyptian dialect in copy for a Jordanian audience.
- Assume email is the primary contact or support channel.
- Western stock photography; imagery that ignores modesty norms.

## References
- Payments: CliQ (instant payments, operated by JoPACC under the Central Bank of Jordan), eFAWATEERcom (bill payment), mobile wallets.
- Government/identity: Sanad (government digital services app).
- Everyday apps users compare to: Talabat, Careem, WhatsApp, local bank apps.
- Data protection: Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 (in force from 2024).

## Tokens
locale ar-JO / en-JO · dir rtl (Arabic) · currency JOD (3 decimals) · week Sun–Thu, weekend Fri–Sat · phone +962 · date DD/MM/YYYY · time 12-hour common in speech, 24-hour acceptable in professional tools · Hijri dates for religious occasions only.

## Checks
- Every price uses JOD with three decimals.
- Every screen passes the RTL mirror test in Arabic.
- Sign-in works with a +962 mobile number.
- Booking/scheduling respects Fri–Sat weekend and Ramadan hours.
