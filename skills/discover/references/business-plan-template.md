# Business plan template

Copy this structure into `docs/01-business-plan.md`. Replace every bracketed note, and delete a section only if the interview gave nothing for it, saying why in one line.

```markdown
# [App name]: business plan

Prepared [date] from an interview with [founder name].

## In one sentence
[Who it is for], [what it lets them do], [unlike what they use today].

## The problem
[2 to 4 sentences, using the founder's own words where possible.]

## Target customers
- **Primary:** [one concrete description]
- **Secondary:** [if any]
- **How many:** [a published figure with its source link, or "no published figure found"]

## Value proposition
[Why a customer would switch from their current workaround. 2 or 3 bullets.]

## Competitors

| Competitor | What it does | Price | Platforms | What users complain about | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [name] | [one line] | [price] | [iOS, Android, web] | [one line] | [link] |

**How we win:** [1 or 2 sentences.]

## Revenue model
[How it makes money, with the price point if the founder gave one.]

## First version: features
Keep this to what the main task needs.

| # | Feature | Why it is in the first version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | [feature] | [reason] |

**Later, not in the first version:** [features the founder deferred]

## Success metrics
- [metric with a number and a date, for example "500 weekly active users by month 6"]

## Risks
| Risk | Why it matters | How we reduce it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [risk] | [impact] | [mitigation] |

## Rules and sensitive data
[Payments, health data, children, location, or user-generated content, or "none identified".]

## Constraints
- Platform: [phone, computer, both]
- Budget for running costs: [amount or "not given"]
- Deadline: [date and reason, or "none"]
- Hard requirements: [exact wording, or "none"]
- Brand feel: [three words]

## Sources
- [every page cited above]
```
